# Maturity Levels (CR-VAS-006)

## Six Levels

Per CR-VAS-006 §5, the maturity model uses six levels. Levels
represent increasing organizational capability, **not** increasing
agenticity.

| Level | Name | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| L0 | Unaware | No deliberate AVS capability. |
| L1 | Aware | AVS concepts understood and identified. |
| L2 | Defined | AVS practices, boundaries and governance are defined. |
| L3 | Implemented | Conformant AVS implementations operate in production. |
| L4 | Managed | AVS performance, risk, value and improvement are systematically managed. |
| L5 | Scaled & Adaptive | AVS capability is governed and continuously optimized across the enterprise. |

## L0: Unaware

The organization has no systematic understanding or deliberate
management of Agentic Value Stream concepts. Agentic behavior may
exist incidentally.

Characteristics:

- No common AVS terminology.
- Agentic implementations may emerge opportunistically.
- No formal semantic boundary.
- No AVS governance.
- No standardized evidence.
- No systematic measurement.
- No capability ownership.

**Important distinction:** an organization at L0 may still operate
something semantically an AVS. The organization simply lacks the
capability to manage it systematically.

## L1: Aware

The organization recognizes Agentic Value Streams as a distinct
architectural and operational concept.

**Minimum capability:** the organization can answer "Where might
agentic participation exist in our Value Streams?" It cannot yet
demonstrate consistent enterprise-wide governance.

## L2: Defined

The organization has established repeatable AVS design,
qualification, evidence, governance, and measurement practices.

**Minimum capability:** the organization can consistently answer
"Is this Value Stream genuinely Agentic, how is the participation
realized, what authority exists, and what evidence supports the
claim?"

**L2 Gate:** defined AVS semantics + defined qualification
approach + authority model + evidence model + measurement model +
governance ownership.

## L3: Implemented

The organization operates conformant Agentic Value Streams in real
operational environments.

**Critical distinction:** a pilot or prototype is NOT automatically
L3. The organization must demonstrate operational realization, not
merely architectural intent.

**L3 Gate:** at least one conformant production AVS + operational
authority enforcement + operational intervention/escalation +
outcome measurement + traceable evidence.

## L4: Managed

The organization systematically manages AVS performance, value,
risk, authority, intervention, and continuous improvement.

**Minimum capability:** the organization can answer "Are our
Agentic Value Streams producing superior or appropriate outcomes,
at acceptable risk and cost, and what should we change?"

**L4 Gate:** repeatable performance management + baseline
comparison + value measurement + risk monitoring + conformance
drift management + systematic improvement.

## L5: Scaled & Adaptive

The organization has enterprise-scale capability to design,
govern, operate, measure, improve, and evolve AVS as a managed
organizational capability.

**Important limitation:** L5 does NOT mean "everything is
autonomous." It means the organization can deliberately and safely
determine where agentic participation creates value, establish
appropriate authority, operate it at scale, measure its effects,
and continuously adapt the capability.

**L5 Gate:** multiple operational AVS implementations + portfolio
governance + reusable capability patterns + enterprise measurement
+ demonstrated scaling + continuous capability evolution.
